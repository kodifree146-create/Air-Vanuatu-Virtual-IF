Rank Structure

Progress through flight hours to unlock expanded aircraft access and routes.

Island Trainee

Unlocks the Dash 8-Q400 for domestic inter-island routes.

0 – 15 Hours

First Officer

Unlocks the Airbus A220-300 for regional Pacific and international flights.

15 – 50 Hours

Captain

Full route network clearance and priority participation in group events.

50 – 120 Hours

Senior Captain

Access to specialized charter routes, testing flights, and event leadership.

120+ Hours
